In addition to performing general web searches using your default search engine, you can also easily search specific websites directly from the Waterfox address bar. Now you can assign a keyword shortcut to your favorite search engines to make it easier to perform a search.

For example, you might assign @shopping to your built-in Amazon search or an abbreviated search engine name:

![search shortcut populate](/assets/docs/2018-11-26-16-11-31-41e2c7.png)

Popular search engine shortcuts will appear on your new tab page as a tile with a magnifying glass. Clicking on these tiles will automatically put @[search shortcut keyword] and a space into your address bar.

## Assign search shortcuts

You can assign search shortcuts to your favorite search engines:

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> and select Settings.
2. Click Search on the left panel.
3. Scroll down to **One-Click Search Engines**.
4. Double-click on the **Keyword** column for the search engine you want to assign a shortcut to.
5. Enter **@** followed by your search shortcut keyword. For example: @ddg for DuckDuckGo.
   ![search shortcut kw 63](/assets/docs/2018-11-26-16-10-37-127b43.png)

Starting in [Waterfox version](/support/find-what-version-waterfox-you-are-using) 82, search shortcut keywords are already included for default search engines.
