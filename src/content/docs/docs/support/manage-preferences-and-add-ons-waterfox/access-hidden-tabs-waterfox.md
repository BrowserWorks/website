Please [update your version of Waterfox](/support/update-waterfox-latest-release) for the latest features and security updates.

## How do I know if I have hidden tabs?

When you hide a tab for the first time using an extension, Waterfox will alert you. From here, you can keep the tab hidden or unhide it and disable the extension.

Clicking <img alt="hidden tabs icon 61" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/2018-06-06-07-29-47-40a658.png"> will also show you what tabs you have hidden.

## How do I access my hidden tabs?

Access your hidden tabs by clicking the <img alt="hidden tabs icon 61" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/2018-06-06-07-29-47-40a658.png"> icon on the toolbar.

![hidden tabs 61](/assets/docs/2018-06-06-12-52-50-7ec254.png)

## How do I find a hidden tab that is playing audio?

When a hidden tab is playing sound, the speaker icon will show up on the hidden tab icon <img alt="hidden tab audio 61" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/2018-06-06-12-49-40-908077.png">. If you click on the icon, the audio indicator will also show which hidden tab from the list is playing audio.

![hidden tab audio list 61](/assets/docs/2018-06-06-12-51-11-56ab49.png)
