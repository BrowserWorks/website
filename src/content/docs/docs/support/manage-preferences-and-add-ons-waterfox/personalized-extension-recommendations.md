---

[Extensions](/support/find-and-install-add-ons-add-features-to-waterfox) are pieces of software, usually created by third-party developers, that you can install to customize your Waterfox browsing experience. You might occasionally see extension recommendations curated through the [Recommended Extensions program](/support/recommended-extensions-program) while you browse in Waterfox and through the Add-ons Manager (about:addons).

![Fx68AddonRecommendations](/assets/docs/2020-06-29-12-04-58-5dfba0.png)

To discover personalized extension recommendations in the Add-ons Manager:

Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" />, click Add-ons and Themes and select Recommendations or Extensions.

## What are personalized extension recommendations in the Add-ons Manager?

Personalized extension recommendations are based on other extensions you’ve installed, profile preferences, hardware, and usage statistics. All user data used to make recommendations is anonymized, non-personal information.

To better predict what extensions you may find interesting, Waterfox uses the Telemetry-Aware Add-on Recommender ([TAAR](https://github.com/mozilla/taar#how-does-it-work)) system—a Waterfox service that recommends extensions by examining basic browser [Telemetry](https://wiki.mozilla.org/Telemetry). This means TAAR analyzes usage statistics from a large number of other Waterfox users, looks at other extensions you may have installed, and considers general characteristics about your Waterfox profile (like language preference). Based on this information, TAAR surfaces extension recommendations tailored just for you.

All the data that TAAR analyzes in order to make personalized extension recommendations is anonymized.

## How are extensions selected?

All extension recommendations appearing in the Add-ons Manager must first pass through a thorough editorial process. Recommended extensions are exceptional extensions that are hand-selected by Waterfox’s editorial team. These extensions meet Waterfox’s highest standards for functionality, user experience, and security.

Extension developers cannot pay for placement in the recommendation program, and Waterfox does not receive any compensation as a result of this process. Recommendations are strictly intended to provide a better browsing experience for Waterfox users.

## How do I opt out of personalized recommendations in the Add-ons Manager?

To turn off personalized recommendations in the Add-ons Manager:

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> and select Settings.
2. In the _Privacy & Security_ panel, scroll down to _Data Collection and Use_ and un-check the box that reads, **Allow Waterfox to make personalized extension recommendations.**

If you opt out, you’ll still see generalized recommended extensions in the Add-ons Manager, however they won’t be personally tailored for you using Telemetry data.

## Who benefits from these personalized recommendations?

Ideally, Waterfox users like you. By providing more tailored extension recommendations, we hope to introduce you to extensions that meet your individual needs.

Recommendations are strictly intended to provide a better browsing experience for Waterfox users. As described above, extension developers cannot pay for placement in the recommendation program, and Waterfox does not receive any compensation as a result of this process.

## How do I learn more about extensions?

Visit [addons.mozilla.org (AMO)](https://addons.mozilla.org) to explore thousands of extensions and themes. Most extensions and themes are created by independent developers from all over world.
