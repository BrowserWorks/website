:::

[Sync](/support/how-do-i-set-sync-my-computer) allows you to sync your bookmarks, logins and browsing information across different devices. When you're no longer using a device, you can remove it from your Mozilla account and delete its associated browsing information, if you choose.

:::note
If you just want to disable Sync for a device without signing out of your Mozilla account, see [Disable Waterfox Sync](/support/disable-waterfox-sync).
:::

1. Open Waterfox on the computer you want to disconnect.
2. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> on the toolbar.
3. Click Mozilla Account (or sign in if your email address isn't showing).
4. Click Manage Account.
5. In the page that opens, scroll down to find the device you want to remove.
6. Click Sign out next to that device.
   - A prompt will ask you to confirm that you want to disconnect.![sign out sync 88](/assets/docs/2021-04-01-15-02-12-47c140.png)
7. Click the Sign out button to confirm

Waterfox will disconnect from your account but won't delete any browsing information.
