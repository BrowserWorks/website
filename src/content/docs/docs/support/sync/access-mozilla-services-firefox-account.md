---

A Mozilla account lets you sign in to services such as Sync to keep your Waterfox data (bookmarks, history, logins, open tabs) available across devices.

## Create or sign in to your Mozilla account

1. Open Menu > Settings.
2. In Sync and save data, click Sign in. Follow the prompts to sign in or create a new account.
3. Confirm your email using the verification link or code sent by Mozilla accounts.

Notes:
- Waterfox uses Mozilla accounts for cross‑device sync. Review the Mozilla Accounts Privacy Notice for details.
- You can choose what to sync: bookmarks, history, logins, open tabs, and more.

## Connect additional devices

- Install Waterfox on your other devices and sign in with the same Mozilla account.
- To send tabs between devices, see [Send Tab from Waterfox desktop to other devices](/support/send-tab-firefox-desktop-other-devices).

## Manage your account

- Visit https://accounts.firefox.com to manage your account details and security.
