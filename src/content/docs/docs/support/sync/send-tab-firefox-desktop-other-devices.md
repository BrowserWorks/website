---

Waterfox’s Send Tab feature lets you send pages from your computer to your other devices (such as an iPhone, iPad or Android device) that are signed in with the same Mozilla account.

:::note
Other “devices” can also mean different Waterfox profiles on the same computer, if you use more than one version or profile. See [Profile Manager — Create, remove or switch Waterfox profiles](/support/profile-manager-create-remove-switch-firefox-profiles).
:::

## Send tabs from your computer

1. Get started by signing in to your [Mozilla account](/support/access-mozilla-services-firefox-account) on your computer. If you don’t have one yet, you can sign up at https://accounts.firefox.com/signup.
2. Sign in to your Mozilla account on your other device as well.
3. Send a tab from your desktop Waterfox:
   - Windows/Linux: Right-click a tab and select Send Tab to Device, then choose the device.
   - macOS: Hold down the Control key while you click a tab and select Send Tab to Device, then choose the device.

## I don’t see my device listed

You’ll be able to send tabs to devices that you’ve added to your Mozilla account. If you don’t see a device listed, add it:

1. Open Waterfox on the device you want to connect.
2. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> to open the menu panel.
3. Select Sign in to Sync or Sync and save data (the exact text may vary by device or version).
4. Follow the prompts to sign in (or sign up) with the same Mozilla account.

Once signed in, your device will sync and appear in the Send Tab list.

To send tabs from your phone or tablet, see:
- [Send a tab from Waterfox for iOS to another device](/support/send-tab-firefox-ios-another-device)
- [Send Tab from another mobile app to Waterfox](/support/send-tab-from-another-mobile-app-to-firefox)
