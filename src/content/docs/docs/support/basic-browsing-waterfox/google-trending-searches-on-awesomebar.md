---

Waterfox can show trending searches from your default search engine when you click the address bar. You can turn this on or off.

## Turn trending searches on or off

1. Open Settings > Privacy & Security.
2. In the Address Bar and Waterfox Suggest sections, adjust the toggles for suggestions and trending content.

Notes:
- Trending searches may not be available for all search engines or regions.
- For details about suggestions, privacy, and sponsored content, see [Waterfox Suggest FAQ](/support/waterfox-suggest-faq) and [Sponsored suggestions privacy](/support/sponsor-privacy).
