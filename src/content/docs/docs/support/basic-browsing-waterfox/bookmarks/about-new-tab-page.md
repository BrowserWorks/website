---

Waterfox makes it easy for you to access great content the moment you open a new tab by displaying your most [frequently and recently](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Frecency) visited sites along with popular web content. The New Tab page is the default Waterfox start page unless you have [set a different home page](/support/how-to-set-the-home-page).

![new tab page 57](/assets/docs/2017-10-11-15-35-09-6b82a5.png)

When you first use Waterfox nothing will be populated. These are eventually replaced with **Top Sites**, which are links to frequently and recently visited sites from your browsing history.

Learn more on how the New Tab page can be customized:

- [Customize your New Tab page to hide or display content](/support/customize-your-new-tab-page)
- [Customize items on your Waterfox New Tab page](/support/customize-items-on-waterfox-new-tab-page)
