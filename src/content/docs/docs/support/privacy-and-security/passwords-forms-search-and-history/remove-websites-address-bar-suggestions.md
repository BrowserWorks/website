---

When you start typing into the address bar, Waterfox displays a drop-down list of suggested sites. This article describes how to clear single items or all items from the address bar history.

- To control what suggestions are shown when you type into the address bar, see [Address bar autocomplete suggestions in Waterfox](/support/address-bar-autocomplete-waterfox) and [Search suggestions in Waterfox](/support/search-suggestions-waterfox).

:::note
Clicking on the address bar displays a drop-down of Shortcuts (most visited sites) by default. See [Customize your Shortcuts](/support/customize-items-on-waterfox-new-tab-page#customize-your-shortcuts) to learn more.
:::

## Clear a single item from the address bar history

This works for both browsing history and search history entries.

1. Type or paste enough text for Waterfox to show the item in the drop-down.
2. Press the ↓ down arrow key to highlight the item.
3. Hold down the Shift key and press Delete on your keyboard.

## Clear all items for a single site

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> to open the menu panel.
   Click History and then click the Manage History bar at the bottom to open the Library window.
2. In the top right corner, type the name of the website you wish to forget in the _Search History_ field, and press Enter.
3. Right-click
   on the site you wish to forget in the resulting list and select Forget About This Site.
   ![History Win6](/assets/docs/2011-02-03-06-25-59-2e196c.png):::note
   Using Forget About This Site will remove all traces of that site from your history. For more information, see [Delete browsing, search and download history on Waterfox](/support/delete-browsing-search-download-history-waterfox).
   :::
4. Close the Library window.

## Clear all items in the address bar history

:::note
If you see items with a star, those are [bookmarks](/support/bookmarks-waterfox) and not part of your browsing history. This is part of the Waterfox [address bar autocomplete](/support/address-bar-autocomplete-waterfox) feature. If you don't want bookmarks or other content to appear in address bar suggestions, see [Changing your address bar settings](/support/address-bar-autocomplete-waterfox#how-can-i-control-what-results-the-address-bar-shows-me).
:::

1. Click on the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> and then click History.
2. Click Clear Recent History… to open the dialog window.
3. In the Clear Recent History window, set **Time range to clear:** to **Everything**.
4. To remove browsing history, make sure **Browsing & Download History** has a check mark next to it. To remove search history, check the box next to **Form & Search History**.
   :::tip
   Be sure to remove the check mark next to any other items that you do not want to be cleared. See [Delete browsing, search and download history on Waterfox](/support/delete-browsing-search-download-history-waterfox) for more information.
   :::

5. To clear all items in the address bar history, click OK.
