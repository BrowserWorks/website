---

Waterfox's built-in [password management](/support/password-manager-remember-delete-edit-logins) lets you access and manage your passwords quickly and securely. When you visit a web page and click on the login field, Waterfox will suggest your saved logins for that page.

1.  Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> to open the menu panel.
2.  Click Logins and Passwords.
3.  Go to the login entry you want to edit or remove (use the search box or scroll through the list on the left).
    ![fx72 lockwise login entries](/assets/docs/2019-12-20-12-42-45-200f6b.png)

        * Click Edit to change your login information.
        * To remove the login information from Waterfox, click the Remove button.![fx72 lockwise edit and delete](/assets/docs/2019-12-20-12-45-30-6601db.png)
