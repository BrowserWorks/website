---

Incorrect system date and time can cause secure connection errors in Waterfox. Certificates appear expired or not yet valid when your clock is wrong.

## Fix your date and time

- Set your device to the correct date, time, and timezone.
- Enable automatic time and timezone if available (Windows, macOS, Linux distributions with NTP).
- Restart Waterfox and reload the page.

## If errors persist

- Check antivirus or security software that intercepts HTTPS and ensure its certificates are valid.
- Review corporate or school proxies/VPNs that inspect traffic; contact your administrator.
- See specific error codes and steps in [Error codes for secure websites](/support/error-codes-secure-websites) and [“Your connection is not secure”](/support/what-does-your-connection-is-not-secure-mean).
