---

This article describes what to do when you can't hear sound in Waterfox.

- To allow autoplay of media with sound, see [Allow or block media autoplay in Waterfox](/support/block-autoplay).

## Check the Volume Mixer

1. Open Waterfox and play content that should play sound, such as this [Waterfox video](http://youtu.be/LtOGa5M8AuU).
2. ClickRight-click the volume icon in the Windows taskbar.
3. Click **Mixer\*\***Open Volume Mixer\*\*. The Volume Mixer window will appear.
4. Make sure the slider for _Waterfox_ is not muted or at the bottom.

## Check your audio device

Make sure that the audio device (such as speakers or headphones) are turned on and connected to the computer properly.

## Other solutions

Check out whether other applications on your computer can play sounds. If they can't:

- Follow Microsoft's [Fix sound problems](https://support.microsoft.com/en-ca/help/4026994/windows-fix-sound-problems) tutorial.

If Waterfox is the only application that you can't hear sound in:

- Try the solutions in [Fix common audio and video issues](/support/fix-common-audio-and-video-issues).
