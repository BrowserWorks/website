title: "Translate websites in Waterfox for Android"
---

Waterfox for Android can translate web pages into your language. Translation happens on your device whenever possible for privacy.

## Translate a page

1. Visit a page in a language different from your device language.
2. If a translation prompt appears, choose your target language and tap Translate.
3. You can also open the menu and look for Translate to open the translation panel.

Notes:
- The current tab remembers your choice until you reload or close it.
- You can switch back to the original at any time from the translation panel.

## Translate selected text

1. Select some text on the page.
2. Tap Translate selection (wording may vary) to translate just the highlighted text.

## Manage translation settings

1. Open Waterfox menu > Settings.
2. In Language or Translations, adjust:
   - Whether to show translation prompts
   - Languages you never want translated
   - Preferred target language

## Manage downloaded languages

- Waterfox downloads translation resources as needed so you can translate even with limited connectivity.
- To remove a language and free space, open Settings > Language/Translations and remove the language downloads.

## Troubleshooting

- No prompt appears on a foreign page
  - Open the translation panel from the menu and choose the language manually.
- A site looks wrong after translation
  - Reload the page, or switch to Show original. You can also disable translation prompts for that site from the panel.
- Language not supported
  - Some languages may not be available yet for on‑device translation.
