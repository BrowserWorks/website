title: "Add the Search bar to your Waterfox toolbar"
---

You can use the Search bar on the [New Tab page](/support/about-new-tab-page) or you can [search the Web from the address bar](/support/search-waterfox-address-bar). You can also [change your search settings in Waterfox](/support/change-your-default-search-settings-waterfox) to add a separate Search bar next to the address bar.

![add search bar 57](/assets/docs/2017-10-21-19-29-29-77093e.gif)

Follow these steps to add a Search bar to your Waterfox toolbar:

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> and select Settings.
2. Click Search on the left panel.
3. Click Add search bar in toolbar.
   ![add search box button 57](/assets/docs/2017-10-26-10-47-44-3ef153.png)
4. Close the _about:preferences_ page. Any changes you've made will automatically be saved.

:::tip
Here's another way to add or remove the Search bar: Click on the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" />, then click Customize….
:::
Drag the search bar into or out of the toolbar and then click Done. See [Customize Waterfox controls, buttons and toolbars](/support/customize-waterfox-controls-buttons-and-toolbars) for details.
