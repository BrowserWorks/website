title: "An extension changed my New Tab page or home page"
---

Extensions (also known as add-ons) are small programs you can install to add features to Waterfox. Some extensions can take control of the home page (what you see when you start Waterfox, open a new window, or click the home icon <img alt="Home Button 57" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/2017-10-19-01-59-26-fcbbdc.png"> in the toolbar) or the [New Tab page](/support/about-new-tab-page) (what you see when you open a new tab). If this happens, Waterfox will ask you when you open it for the first time if that change is okay.

![Fx83NewTabChange-MomentumAddon](/assets/docs/2020-12-19-17-53-55-1682ec.png)

## View and disable the extension

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" /> and select Settings.
2. Click **Home**.
3. Under **New Windows and Tabs**, it will indicate which extension has changed your home page or New Tab page.
   ![DisableExtensionNewTab](/assets/docs/2020-04-01-13-02-45-164cdc.png)
4. Click Disable Extension to prevent the extension from changing your home or New Tab page again. The page will change back to what you had previously set. To change your home page again, see [How to set the home page](/support/how-to-set-the-home-page).

## Remove the extension

1. Click the menu button <img alt="menu button" class="inline-image" src="/assets/docs/icons/menu.svg" />, click Add-ons and Themes and select Extensions.
2. Under **Manage Your Extensions** find the extension you want to remove.
3. On the right side of the extension click the menu (three dots) and select Remove.
   ![fx72 remove extensions](/assets/docs/2019-12-20-16-33-40-a77f48.png)
