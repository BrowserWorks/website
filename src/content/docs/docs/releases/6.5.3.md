### Fixes

- Fixed various theming issues.
- Updated base configurations, removing unused values.
- Latest security fixes[^1].

### Known Issues

- The Restart button is missing on Linux and Windows.
- Some users may experience that their session may not be able to be restored (your sessions are still in your profile, so your data is still available). May be related to [Bug 1901899](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1901899).
