### Fixes

- Updated the JPEG-XL library to the latest version. This should stop various websites that serve `.jxl` files from crashing in the tab they're loaded.
- The missing strings for Look & Feel page are now no longer missing, meaning you can change your theme preferences once again.


### Known Issues

- The Restart button is missing on Linux and Windows.
- The status bar colour does not match whichever theme colours are set on Linux and Windows.
- Some user's may experience that their session may not be able to be restored (your sessions is still in your profile, so the data is still available). May be related to [Bug 1901899](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1901899).
